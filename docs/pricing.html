Pricing & in-app purchase policy

This page describes the one-time digital products and their verified fulfillment. It does not quote a currency price or claim that a Google Play offer is live until it is available in the app.

Family edition purchases

Google Play presents the real-money price before payment. Children should ask a parent or guardian. Purchases are server-verified; ads use child-directed treatment for every player. An offer is available only when its product and region are enabled by Google Play.

Where to see your price

Google Play supplies the localized price and currency shown in the app and in its purchase confirmation. Your final price, currency, product availability and applicable tax treatment depend on Google Play and the country or region for your account. Check the purchase confirmation before paying. This website does not take payments or offer a separate web checkout.

How prices and purchases work

  • The app uses the price returned by Google Play Billing for the available product. It does not calculate or promise live currency conversion, invent a local price, or collect payment on this website.
  • Gem packs are consumable in-game items. Gems are virtual game currency and are not a cash balance or a cash-redemption service.
  • remove_ads is a non-consumable entitlement. It suppresses eligible non-rewarded ads; the player may still choose an opt-in rewarded ad where that feature is available.
  • The server verifies one-time purchases before granting Gems or Remove Ads. Pending, invalid, mismatched or unverified transactions must not create an entitlement. Duplicate callbacks and retries must not double-grant.
  • Purchase availability may depend on Google Play account eligibility, the selected country or region, the product's activation and the app's distribution availability. If a price is unavailable, the app should not invent one.

Orders, cancellations and support

Revoked purchases are periodically reconciled: revoked Remove Ads access is removed unless another valid purchase grants it; refunded Gems are deducted from the remaining balance without making it negative. Already-used cosmetics are not automatically removed. Contact support to dispute an adjustment.

Google Play handles the purchase flow and retains its own order/payment history. Use Google Play order history and its official support/refund process for an order or refund request; eligibility is determined under Google Play's applicable rules. If an order is confirmed by Google Play but an in-game entitlement has not arrived, contact the publisher using the support contact configured on this site. Never send passwords, account tokens, purchase tokens, full payment details or guest deletion codes to support.

For data handling, account deletion and provider details, read the Privacy Policy. For account deletion, use the official account and data deletion page.

Support

For a billing or missing-entitlement issue, contact AI@rekixo.com. Use the same Google Play order reference shown in Play, but do not send purchase tokens or authentication credentials.
