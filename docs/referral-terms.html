Who receives the reward?

The inviter can receive Pearl Paw Dice, Blossom Garden Board, Rabbit and 100 Gems once in campaign friendship-bundle-v1. The invited player receives no referral reward. Sharing, clicking, installing or opening the app alone does not earn a reward. The new friend does not have to win. Already-owned cosmetics stay owned; they are not duplicated, refunded or exchanged for extra Gems. The additional 100 Gems are granted once even if some or all cosmetics are already owned. Rewards have no cash value and are not transferable outside the game.

How to qualify

  1. The inviter links an eligible Google account and explicitly accepts the current invitation disclosure. Optional Google services retain the game's neutral privacy-screen eligibility; guest gameplay is unaffected.
  2. The inviter voluntarily shares their own invitation with a genuine friend using Android's share chooser. Do not spam strangers, buy installs/accounts, share credentials or manipulate Play rankings, ratings or reviews.
  3. A genuinely new player uses the invitation landing page's Google Play download button and starts a licensed Play installation within seven days of that download click. Existing installs, self-invites, replayed links/claims and account/reinstall farming do not qualify.
  4. Before the qualifying match, the new player links an eligible Google account, opens Home → Share, reads the disclosure and chooses Verify my invitation. Both participation and a valid invitation installation must be verified. If no valid referrer exists, an old installation cannot be retroactively converted to a referral.
  5. The new player completes a natural Online or Friends match within fourteen days of the installation timestamp and before the campaign closes. It must last at least three minutes from server start, include at least one legal move by the referred player, have no bots or forfeited players, and end normally with all four winner tokens home. Offline, Team Up, abandoned and fabricated matches do not qualify.

Verification, withdrawal and support

The backend—not a local counter or share callback—determines eligibility and atomically grants the configured items and Gems. Network outages, provider checks or a full Gem wallet can delay delivery. A server-confirmed reward is safe to retry without duplicate grants. Use Refresh progress to check delivery; a stale local wallet may need a later sync. Verification does not guarantee a unique human or eliminate every type of fraud.

You can decline verification and still play normally. Stop optional invitation verification stops new verification on the device and requests removal of current participation and unqualified incoming attribution on the backend. If offline, local verification stops first; retry Stop to confirm server cleanup. A previously bound invitation cannot be reused. Deleting an account or withdrawing before qualification is confirmed can cancel unverified progress. Already-earned rewards are not removed just for withdrawing; account deletion removes the deleted account's data and entitlements under the deletion policy.

For a missing or disputed reward, contact AI@rekixo.com with the campaign name and approximate date. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, purchase tokens, private keys or guest deletion codes. Individual eligibility and suspected abuse can be reviewed through support. These terms do not exclude rights that applicable consumer or privacy law gives you.

Privacy and retention

Verification uses account links, Play click/install timestamps, temporary app/device integrity tokens, participation/consent records and server match results. Google Play verifies integrity tokens; referral code does not persist or log them. No contact-list permission, advertising ID or device fingerprint is requested. An authenticated consent-version record is a user choice, not independent proof of age or parental consent. Provider privacy/retention rules are separate.

Click records are retained up to 21 days, challenges five minutes and accepted match references fourteen days. Minimal queued terminal-match delivery records can survive ordinary room cleanup during an outage, no later than fourteen days after the ninety-day campaign ends. They contain only the identifiers and authoritative finish evidence needed for retry, not chat or authentication credentials. Minimal participant Google-subject history/anti-replay hashes and campaign attribution are purged at that same campaign deadline. Reward/idempotency records stay with an active account. Account deletion removes coordinator participation, invitations, claim relationships, accepted match references and awards. Previously queued minimal room outbox evidence can remain until its bounded campaign deadline; deletion markers prevent it recreating attribution or rewards. Limited anti-replay hashes and minimal deletion markers remain as described in the privacy policy.
