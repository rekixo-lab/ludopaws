LUDO PAWS · REKIXO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Ludo Paws is a mobile Ludo-style game published under the Rekixo brand. This policy explains what the game and its online services may process, why they process it, which service providers may receive it, how long selected records are kept, and how to request account deletion. The data processed depends on the features you use and the services enabled in the version you play.

Family edition

The current family edition is intended for children and adults. It offers offline play, online multiplayer, Friends invitations and fixed quick-chat reactions. Public player names are generated by the server; your device nickname is not sent as your online name. Free-text messaging, microphone/voice chat, advertising, purchases and new Google account linking are unavailable in this edition. It does not request a date of birth or Google Play age-range sharing. Existing account recovery and deletion remain available.

Before worldwide publication, the publisher must approve the actual child-data processing, any required parental-consent arrangements, provider configuration, support process and store disclosures. These source restrictions alone do not certify compliance in every country.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account and identity: an internal account identifier, display name, account type and status, and links to a Google identity, Friend ID or leaderboard identity when you use those features. The account service uses a hash of the Google identity subject as its account-linking key; Ludo Paws does not need your Google password.
  • Authentication and security: session identifiers, session expiry/revocation metadata, refresh-token hashes, sign-in nonces, request identifiers, and limited security/rate-limit records used to authenticate requests, prevent replay and abuse, rotate or revoke sessions, and keep the service available.
  • Guest deletion and anti-replay records: a guest account may use an installation recovery request key and an optional 64-character delete-only code. The server stores hashes of these values, not the displayed deletion code itself, and may keep minimal account/deletion tombstones to prevent a deleted account or credential from being re-created or replayed.
  • Gameplay and fairness: online match identifiers, pseudonymous player identifiers, player seats/colours, game status, moves and outcomes, timestamps, server dice receipts, ruleset identifiers and event history used to run games, provide fairness details and investigate integrity issues. Offline games may keep local progress and game state on your device.
  • Voice: the family edition does not capture microphone audio or establish WebRTC voice connections. The microphone permission and Android WebRTC dependency have been removed.
  • Friends and social features: Friend IDs, generated public names, friendship requests and relationships, invitations and recent-player entries. New free-text conversations are unavailable. Legacy conversation records from older editions may remain until deletion under the retention rules below.
  • Leaderboard and progression: a linked leaderboard profile identifier, display name, submitted match results, wins, scores or points, and local profile/progression statistics.
  • Store and entitlements: gem balance, selected and owned cosmetics, cosmetic unlock progress, rewarded-ad progress when enabled, Play product identifiers, and hashed transaction or purchase references used to verify entitlements and prevent duplicate rewards. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not requested by Ludo Paws.
  • Device-local information: settings, selected appearance, offline game state, profile statistics and account-scoped cache data stored by the app. Some local data can remain on the device if the app cannot finish local cleanup after a server deletion.
  • Technical connection information: our hosting, network and service providers may process an IP address, request metadata and device/network information to route traffic, protect systems and diagnose failures. The API derives a one-way hash from client IP information for rate limiting and does not intentionally save raw IP addresses in match or rate-limit state; providers may have their own operational logs and retention rules.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and restore accounts; authenticate and secure sessions; provide offline and online gameplay; operate friends, messages, invitations and leaderboards; synchronize account-backed progression and cosmetic entitlements; verify purchases and rewarded-ad callbacks; preserve game-integrity evidence; prevent fraud, replay and duplicate grants; troubleshoot outages; and respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.

3. Third-party services and sharing

Depending on the features enabled, Ludo Paws uses Cloudflare hosting and Durable Objects for API, account, social, match and signaling storage; server-generated online dice; Google identity services for sign-in; Google Play Games Services for supported achievements/leaderboard integration; Google Play for app distribution and one-time in-app purchases; Google's public STUN service for optional Friends voice-chat connection setup; and Google Mobile Ads/AdMob and Google's consent tools when advertising is enabled. In the current voice-chat client, the signaling service forwards connection-negotiation data; voice audio is sent to participating peers over WebRTC rather than being uploaded as a game API recording. These providers receive or process data only as needed to deliver their services, protect systems, verify transactions or rewards, or meet legal requirements. In optional voice chat, the game uses microphone audio only after the player starts voice chat and grants the Android microphone permission. Each provider has its own privacy notice and terms. Check the provider's notice for its independent processing and retention practices.

Ludo Paws does not sell personal information. It does not ask players to send passwords, access/refresh tokens, purchase tokens or guest deletion codes to support. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent abuse, resolve security incidents or enforce legitimate claims.

4. Google Play purchases and advertising

The one-time product contract and Gem amounts are listed in the Pricing and in-app purchase policy. Google Play supplies localized checkout prices and currency; the website does not offer a separate checkout or promise a fixed regional price. The server verifies each Google Play one-time purchase before granting its configured Gems or non-consumable entitlement. Hashed purchase references and account-bound records help prevent duplicate grants. Deleting a Ludo Paws account removes associated app-side purchase references where the service can identify and delete them; it does not delete Google's independent order, payment, tax or refund records.

When advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob may process ad-request information, device or advertising identifiers, consent signals and ad-interaction information under its own policies. A player chooses whether to watch an opt-in rewarded ad. The configured reward is five Gems, and it is granted only after the server-side reward verification succeeds; eligible cosmetic-targeted rewards may also update cosmetic progress. The Remove Ads purchase suppresses eligible non-rewarded ads, but does not prevent a player from choosing a rewarded ad. Google consent tools may present consent or privacy options where required. Optional Friends voice chat requires the user to start the feature and grant microphone permission; the current game client does not intentionally record or store voice-chat audio on the game server. These disclosures describe feature processing and do not certify that all monetization has passed its release tests.

5. Local storage and security

The app uses Android Keystore-backed protection for account credentials and selected sensitive local records where implemented. Ludo Paws API requests use HTTPS, and server-side verification—not a local wallet cache or a client-only callback—is used for paid entitlements. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and users should keep devices updated, protect their Google account and never share credentials or deletion codes.

6. Retention

Account, profile, wallet, cosmetics, social, leaderboard and session data is retained while the account and associated features are active, and is deleted through the account-deletion process described below when the request completes, subject to the limited records described in this section. Short-lived sessions also expire or can be revoked.

Online match records: No separate long-term match-proof archive is created by the current online protocol. The active match room stores game state and recent command receipts for gameplay, reconnects and retries, then removes inactive or completed rooms through its scheduled cleanup.

Limited deletion/security markers: minimal account-deletion tombstones and hashed guest request/deletion credentials may be retained for as long as needed to prevent a deleted account or credential from being reactivated or replayed. These are security/replay-prevention records, not active player profiles, and are not used to serve ads. The current implementation does not assign a fixed expiry to every such marker.

Google Play retains its separate order history under Google's practices. Third-party providers may retain operational logs under their own policies. A local offline game or local cache may remain on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app; server-side deletion does not remotely erase every copy of information already stored on your device or by an independent provider.

7. How to delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile → menu → Delete account and data and confirm the permanent action. The server deletion flow removes the account profile, sessions, wallet and gem balance, cosmetics and rewarded-ad progress, account-linked app purchase references, Google identity link, linked Friend ID/social records, and linked leaderboard profile. The app also attempts to clear the matching local profile, wallet, pending-purchase, friend and leaderboard caches.

Outside the app: use the official account and data deletion page. A Google-linked account requires verification with the Google identity previously linked to Ludo Paws and a separate confirmation. A guest-only account can be deleted using the optional delete-only code created in the app before uninstalling. The code can delete an eligible unlinked guest account only; it cannot sign in or recover progress. Keep it private and enter it only on the official deletion page.

If server deletion succeeds but the app reports a local cleanup problem, clear Ludo Paws app storage on that device before giving the device to someone else. If you no longer have a guest code or cannot complete verification, contact AI@rekixo.com. Support will verify ownership through a reasonable process. Do not email passwords, access/refresh tokens, purchase tokens, full payment details or deletion codes.

Deleting a Ludo Paws account does not delete your Google account, Google's Play order history, or separate data already controlled by an independent provider. The match-record and security-marker retention described in Section 6 also applies after account deletion.

8. Children and intended audience

The intended audience includes children and adults. Public communications are limited to fixed reactions and generated names; voice and free-text messaging are unavailable. The publisher must complete the target-audience declaration, child-directed privacy review, applicable consent arrangements and Google Play Families review before public release.

9. Your choices and contact

You can choose whether to use online multiplayer and Friends features. New Google identity linking is unavailable in the family edition. You may request account deletion as described above. For privacy questions, correction requests or a deletion request that cannot be completed through the available flow, contact the publisher at AI@rekixo.com.
